# Mood board notes (from Peter's 16 reference photos, 2026-10-24)

Photos live in `design/private/reference/` (not committed). Used as **reference only** for now, not as backgrounds.

What to take from them:
- **Low camera angles at ball height**: ball in focus at the front, the pitch or a player blurred behind. Use for hero framing and 3D camera angles.
- **Night floodlights**: one hard light source, deep black, green turf glowing. A non-red version of the night mood.
- **Motion blur on the shot**: a fast streak with a sharp ball. The direction for the slam and kick-off transitions.
- **Gear on turf**: discs, cones, hurdles, balls lined up. Kit check and empty states.
- **Graphic lines**: white lane lines on black, chalk lines on grass. Use as abstract background texture and dividers.
- **Daylight versions** (blue sky, sun flare, foggy morning pitch): the light-mode mood.
- **Real game moments** (1v1, pressing): drill-card imagery for what the drill trains.

Rejected by Peter: the red sky / red-lit goal scene (mockups E and F).
